About CryPrice

CryPrice is a read-only DeFi risk intelligence project for public address monitoring, portfolio visibility, and market risk context. The service helps users monitor Aave Health Factor, protocol exposure, and portfolio risk across Ethereum, Arbitrum, Avalanche, and Base using public addresses they choose to track.

CryPrice is informational software: a read-only monitoring stack — not a wallet, exchange, custodian, or transaction service. Account access and the interactive dashboard are provided on app.cryprice.dev.
